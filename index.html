One-way electron mirrors from single-layer stacking shifts in rhombohedral graphite

Executed Jupyter notebooks that generate the data panels of the paper's figures from the data set published on Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23419464). Preprint: arXiv:2610.20905. The notebooks themselves are in the GitHub repository.

  • Fig. 1b-h – dI/dV spectra versus tight-binding DOS, dI/dV maps at several biases
  • Fig. 2 – line section and its derivative, scattering wave vectors on the TB bands, line spectrum across a wall, TB LDOS section
  • Fig. 3 – layer-resolved tight-binding LDOS
  • Fig. 4 – DFT bands with layer participation number and wave-packet populations, charge densities, wave-packet reflection
  • Fig. S2 – further line spectra across domain walls
  • Fig. S3 – rhombohedral ribbon, experiment and TB
  • Fig. S4 – TB LDOS without doping and with doping of the opposite sign
  • Fig. S6 – wave-packet reflection for top- and bottom-layer leads
  • Fig. S7c,d – minimal scattering model
